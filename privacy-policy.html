Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens when you use our QR scanner.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Camera access

If you press “Start camera”, your browser asks for permission to use your camera. The camera is used to scan QR codes while scanning is active. You can stop the camera or remove its permission in your browser settings.

Images and QR results

When you choose a QR image, the website uses JavaScript in your browser to read it. This website's code does not send your selected image or decoded QR result to a website-owned server. The result is shown on your screen so you can copy it or choose to open a detected web link.

Do not scan or share sensitive information unless you understand what the QR code contains. If you open an external link, that website has its own privacy practices.

Third-party library

This website loads the html5-qrcode scanning library from the unpkg content delivery network. When your browser requests this library, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. This external service is needed for the scanner to work. Please also review the provider's own policies.

Website hosting and technical logs

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visits to GitHub Pages sites can result in visitors' IP addresses being logged and stored for security purposes. These logs are handled by the hosting provider under its own policies.

Cookies and advertising

This starter website does not intentionally set its own advertising or analytics cookies. If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added in the future, this policy must be updated to explain the services and data they use.

If Google AdSense is added, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to the applicable settings and policies. We will update this policy before enabling those services. Visitors can learn more about Google's data practices at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

This website is a general-purpose QR scanning tool and does not ask visitors to submit personal details to use the scanner. Parents or guardians should guide children when opening links from QR codes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page if the website changes. The date at the top will be updated when a significant change is made. Please check this page from time to time.

Contact us

If you have a privacy question, use the details on our Contact Us page.
