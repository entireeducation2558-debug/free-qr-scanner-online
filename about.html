A simple website to help you scan QR codes.

About this website

QR Scanner is a free online tool. You can use your device camera or upload a QR code image to read the information inside it.

You can copy the result or choose to open a web link. Please check a link before opening it, because a QR code can lead to an unsafe website.

Our aim

Our aim is to make QR code scanning simple and easy to use. We want visitors to understand how the tool works and use it carefully.
