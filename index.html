How to scan a QR code

  1. Choose Scan with camera and allow camera access, or choose Upload image.
  2. For camera scanning, hold the QR code steady and keep it well lit.
  3. When a result appears, review it before copying or opening any link.

What can a QR code contain?

A QR code may contain a website address, plain text, contact details, Wi-Fi information, or other encoded data. This tool displays the decoded content it can read; it does not verify that the content or destination is safe.

Privacy at a glance

Camera access is used only after you press the start button and grant browser permission. Image decoding is performed in your browser using a third-party JavaScript library. This site’s code does not upload selected QR images to a server. Your browser and the library provider may have their own technical behavior and policies; read the full Privacy Policy.
