How to Scan a QR Code

A simple guide to scanning a QR code and using the result safely.

Scan with your camera

  1. Open the QR Scanner tool.
  2. Press “Start camera” and allow camera access when your browser asks.
  3. Hold the QR code steady in good light and keep the full code inside the frame.
  4. Read the result before copying it or opening a link.

Scan from an image

  1. Choose the “Upload image” tab.
  2. Select a clear image with the whole QR code visible.
  3. Wait for the decoded result. If it fails, try a sharper image without glare.

If it does not work

  • Make sure your internet connection is working so the scanning library can load.
  • Camera scanning generally needs HTTPS or localhost. Allow camera permission in your browser.
  • Try better lighting, a cleaner camera lens, or a clearer image.

Stay safe

QR codes may lead to fake or unsafe websites. Check the website address carefully. Do not enter passwords, payment information, or personal details on a website you do not trust. This scanner reads QR content but cannot confirm whether it is safe.
